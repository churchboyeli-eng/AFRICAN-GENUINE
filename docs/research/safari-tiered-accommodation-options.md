# Tanzania tiered accommodation options — sourcing note

**Research date:** 20 August 2026
**Scope:** named safari regions in the current catalogue. This is a planning shortlist based on first-party property or operator sources; it is **not** a supplier agreement, live inventory feed, price promise, or permission to reuse a property's media.

## How to use this note

- **Mid-range** and **luxury** are planning labels for African Genuine Tours & Safaris, not classifications claimed by every owner. Confirm the current quote, room/tent category, route logistics and opening dates before naming a stay in a customer proposal.
- Treat every option as **illustrative and subject to confirmation**. Do not publish live availability, rooms remaining, instant confirmation, contracted rates or supplier status without an authorised inventory/allotment feed.
- A property page, gallery or media kit is **not** a licence to reuse its photographs. Use only assets owned or licensed in writing for African Genuine Tours & Safaris; do not hotlink or download property images from these sources.
- “Unsupported” means this pass did not establish a precise named property from a sufficiently clear official owner/operator source. It is deliberately not a gap to fill with an OTA, review site or guess.

## Publishing rules for the site

Until African Genuine Tours & Safaris holds written supplier terms and valid image permission, customer-facing safari pages should say one of:

- “Selected camp or lodge, confirmed with your quote.”
- “Illustrative mid-range or luxury stay, subject to confirmation.”

They should not say:

- “Live availability,” “rooms left,” “guaranteed hotel,” or “instant confirmation.”
- That a camp's third-party photographs are African Genuine Tours & Safaris media.

## Evidence boundary

The links above establish the properties' own descriptions and locations where stated. They do **not** establish current availability, agency contracts, commission, rates, room allotments, transfer feasibility for a particular date, or permission to reuse images. Those require a direct, dated commercial confirmation from the property, owner, appointed DMC or authorised bed-bank.
